## It's not really that hard

- Computers use sequences of numbers to represent
    - **inputs** like mouse and MIDI control interactions
    - **outputs** like audio, pixels, and MIDI
- All transform numbers into other numbers. **Thats it!**

```python
# Convert text to numbers
[ord(c) for c in "hello"] 

# Convert numbers to text
"".join(chr(n) for n in [104, 101, 108, 108, 111])
```

- You can think of programming as a series of tricks that enable programmers to **NOT** have to think about numbers.
