1. `cd /ada-lovelace/assignment-5`
2. `cd ada-lovelace/assignment-5`
3. `cd ~/ada-lovelace/assignment-5`

<Toggle title="Answer">

1. `cd ..` (do this twice)

<Toggle title="Answer">

**2.** Run `cd ..` three times: up to `ada-lovelace`, then `lmsc261-homework`, then `GitHub`.
